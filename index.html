Why CCTV sits on its own /26 Security camera traffic is isolated so a compromised admin device can't reach surveillance feeds, and so continuous video traffic can't crowd out other zones' bandwidth. Why comms gets a /25 Ship-to-shore communication needs headroom for burst traffic during berthing and cargo handoffs, so it's sized larger than admin despite fewer fixed devices. Why cargo yard is a /24 The largest device count — handheld scanners, yard cameras, crane controllers — needs the most address space of any zone. Why everything meets at one core A single core switch keeps routing between zones centrally controlled, so access rules between zones are enforced in one place, not scattered across the network.
